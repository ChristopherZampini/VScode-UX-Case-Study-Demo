Problem

Readiness work gets scattered.

The operational challenge is not just finding requirements; it is maintaining a clear, reviewable line from scope to implementation and evidence.

THE PROBLEM

Requirements, policies, and evidence live apart.

It is hard to see what is done, what is missing, and who owns the next step.

THE GOAL

Connect the work to the assessment.

Make each practice traceable to an owner, policy, and supporting evidence.

Approach

Build from the Level 1 Assessment Guide.

Start with the supplied source, define the system boundary, and translate requirement objectives into a manageable operational workflow.

Insight

A checklist alone is not an SSP.

The guide evaluates implementation through objectives and evidence. The tool connects those pieces to the real work—and keeps the workflow adaptable.

For every requirement, the preparation process should clarify the relevant system boundary, what is implemented, who owns it, and where reliable evidence is maintained.

A useful workspace is more than a static list: it helps people resolve open questions, keep policy language aligned with practice, and prepare records for qualified review.
