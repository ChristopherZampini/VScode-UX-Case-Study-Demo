End-to-end flow

Connect the record, not just the checklist.

The prototype is designed to make handoffs and missing information visible, rather than implying that a status alone proves implementation.

Scope the boundary

Identify the system, assets, users, and information in scope with the right stakeholders.

Assign the work

Translate requirements into practices with a named owner and a useful next action.

Document the practice

Draft policy language and capture how the organization actually implements the practice.

Attach and review

Connect supporting evidence, confirm ownership, and keep review dates visible.

Important boundary: This is an illustrative preparation prototype, not an authoritative SSP, assessor finding, or compliance determination. Do not enter CUI or sensitive evidence. Validate scope, implementation, policy, evidence handling, and assessment conclusions with the organization’s approved process and qualified stakeholders.
